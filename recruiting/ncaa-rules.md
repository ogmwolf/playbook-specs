# ncaa-rules.md — NCAA Volleyball Recruiting Rules Reference

## Key dates for Class of 2028 (current freshmen, born ~2010)

### DI Men's Volleyball

- Coaches may NOT directly contact recruits until: **June 15 after sophomore year (June 15, 2026)**
- Before June 15, 2026: coaches can send camp brochures, non-athletic institutional info, NCAA educational materials only
- Athletes can contact coaches at ANY time — no restrictions
- Official visits: not until August 1 before junior year (August 1, 2026)
- Verbal offers: after June 15 sophomore year
- National Letter of Intent: mid-November senior year

### What Jake and Wyatt can do RIGHT NOW (freshman, spring 2026)

- Email coaches directly (no restrictions on athlete outreach)
- Fill out recruiting questionnaires on program websites
- Attend ID camps (coaches can observe)
- Have coaches watch them at club tournaments (evaluation periods)
- Send Hudl links to coaches

### What DI coaches can do right now

- Watch Jake and Wyatt at tournaments
- Communicate with their club/HS coaches
- Send camp information and non-athletic materials
- Cannot call, text, or email Jake/Wyatt directly until June 15, 2026

## 2025–26 DI Men's Volleyball Recruiting Calendar key periods

Contact period: coaches may have in-person contact with recruits off-campus
Quiet period: coaches may not have in-person contact off-campus
Dead period: no in-person contact at all

Check NCAA.org for exact dates — these change year to year.

## Position-specific recruiting notes

### Libero (Jake)

- Typically evaluated later than front-row players (height not a factor)
- Key metrics coaches track: pass average (target 2.3+), serve receive %, floor coverage
- DI libero spots are limited (typically 1–2 per roster)
- Watch for "libero openings by class year" on VBNerd.com

### Setter / Opposite (Wyatt)

- 6'3" is excellent height for a setter at DI level
- Setter/opposite flexibility is a significant recruiting asset
- Setters typically recruited by junior year
- Key metrics: set efficiency %, court vision, serving
