# vision.md — Playbook Long-term Vision

## The long game

Playbook and Scoutly are complementary products targeting the same market from different angles.

Scoutly: athlete-facing, public, social — "LinkedIn for youth sports"
Playbook: parent-facing, private, operational — "family command center"

In v3+, they connect: parent manages private recruiting data in Playbook, athlete manages public profile in Scoutly. One ecosystem, two products.

## The Advisor is the moat

Every other recruiting tool (NCSA, FieldLevel, Hudl) is a database or a network. None of them have a contextual AI advisor that knows your specific kid.

The Advisor — powered by Claude with the family's real data as context — is the feature no competitor can easily replicate. It's not just AI, it's AI that already knows Jake's pass average, his school list, and that he has a Big West ID Camp in 17 days.

That's the defensible position.

## The email CRM opportunity

Upload + compose + send + track opens is essentially what NCSA charges families $3,000–5,000 for. If Playbook builds that well, it's a genuine differentiator and a monetization path.

v3 feature. Needs backend first.
